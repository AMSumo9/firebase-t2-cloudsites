Car Finance Wollongong Comparison Explained

Choosing the right structure starts with understanding the security the lender requires. A secured car loan uses the vehicle as collateral. Because the lender can repossess the car if repayments stop, they generally offer lower interest rates. These loans are typically available for newer vehicles purchased from recognised dealers, and the lender will register an interest on the Personal Property Securities Register (PPSR).

Unsecured personal loans do not require an asset as security. This offers flexibility, allowing you to purchase older vehicles or buy from private sellers, but it carries a higher interest rate. Lenders view these as higher risk, so the trade-off is a higher cost of borrowing over the loan term.

How lenders assess your application

Lenders in the Illawarra region look at several key factors when deciding whether to approve your car loan. Your credit history is a primary indicator of reliability, while income stability ensures you can manage the repayments. Existing debts are considered to calculate your debt-to-income ratio, ensuring the new loan does not overextend you financially.

The loan-to-value ratio (LVR) is also critical. A larger deposit lowers the LVR, which can secure a better interest rate. Conversely, opting for a balloon payment at the end of the term reduces your monthly repayments but requires a lump sum payment later. New versus used vehicle finance

New car buyers often encounter manufacturer-backed finance at dealerships. These rates can be competitive during promotional periods but restrict you to that specific brand. This limits your ability to compare the broader market. Used car finance offers more freedom, allowing you to shop across dealerships and private sales.

However, lenders impose age limits on vehicles for secured loans. If the car will be too old by the end of the loan term, you may only qualify for an unsecured loan with a higher rate. A broker can check eligibility before you apply, saving time and protecting your credit score. For business owners, commercial products like chattel mortgages offer tax benefits, treating the vehicle as a business asset.

Common questions

Is a deposit required for car finance? A deposit is not always mandatory, but contributing a deposit reduces the amount you need to borrow. This lowers your loan-to-value ratio (LVR), which can help you secure a lower interest rate and reduce your total interest payable over the life of the loan.

Can I get finance for a used car? Yes, finance is available for used cars. However, lenders usually have age limits on the vehicle. If the car is too old, you may need to apply for an unsecured personal loan rather than a secured car loan. A broker can check which lenders accept the specific age and model you are considering.

How long does car finance approval take? Approval times vary by lender and your personal situation. Unconditional approval can sometimes be received within 24 to 48 hours if your documentation is complete. Using a broker can speed up the process as they know exactly what each lender requires and can address issues early.
