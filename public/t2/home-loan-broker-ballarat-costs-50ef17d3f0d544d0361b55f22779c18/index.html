Home Loan Broker Ballarat Costs Explained

When you use a home loan broker in Ballarat, the service is completely free to you. Mortgage brokers are paid a commission by the lender when your loan settles. This does not affect your interest rate or loan terms. Since January 2021, mortgage brokers have been legally required to act in your best interests, not the lender's. This means they must prioritise your outcome, providing transparent, unbiased advice backed by law. You do not pay for this advice out of pocket. The broker's fee is baked into the lender's margin. This structure aligns the broker's incentives with yours, as they want you to secure a loan you can afford and keep, rather than just pushing a product for a quick commission. Because brokers are paid by the lender, they can access products that might not be available directly to consumers. This includes niche products for specific professions or investment strategies. The broker acts as a filter, saving you from applying to lenders who are unlikely to approve your application, which can damage your credit file.

What you actually pay

While the broker fee is zero, there are other costs involved in buying a home. This scheme is available to all eligible buyers from October 2025. It is important to factor these costs into your budget before you apply.
