Common questions

What does a finance broker in Canberra do? A broker helps you compare lender policies, prepare documents and understand borrowing options. Credit advice is provided by a licensed broker or authorised credit representative.

Is the ACT Home Buyer Concession Scheme income tested? ACT Revenue Office guidance states that from 1 July 2026 there is no income threshold for the Home Buyer Concession Scheme, but you should always check the current page before signing.

How much deposit do I need in Canberra? Deposit needs depend on property price, lender policy, savings history and whether a guarantee or concession applies. Many buyers compare 5, 10 and 20 percent pathways.
