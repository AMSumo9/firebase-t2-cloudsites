Bathroom Renovations Cairns Costs Explained

When planning a bathroom upgrade in Cairns, the price is driven by three main factors: the extent of layout changes, the level of waterproofing required, and any custom joinery or premium fixtures. A compact refresh that keeps the existing footprint typically sits at the lower end of the market, while a full strip-to-stud renovation with bespoke cabinetry can push costs toward the upper end of the $65,000+ range. Typical timelines by project type

Project duration varies widely based on scope and complexity. A refresh that updates surfaces, fixtures, vanity, tapware and lighting while keeping the existing layout usually completes in 2 to 3 weeks. Mid-range renovations that involve layout changes, plumbing relocation and full tiling typically run 4 to 8 weeks from strip-out to handover. Luxury or custom builds with stone, custom joinery or frameless glass can extend to around 10 weeks. A clear timeline should be included in the written Domestic Building Contract to avoid surprises. Licensing, contracts and warranty

In Queensland, any residential building work over $3,300 including labour and materials must be carried out by a QBCC‑licensed contractor under a written Domestic Building Contract. A bathroom job that moves walls, changes drainage or affects waterproofing generally falls into this regulated category, so checking a contractor's licence status matters before signing anything. Work over that threshold also carries QBCC Home Warranty cover, paid for by the contractor. Structural defects are covered for 6 years and 6 months, non‑structural defects for 6 months. Any notifiable plumbing work still needs a licensed plumber and a lodged QBCC Form 4, even inside a smaller refresh.

Designing for Cairns' tropical climate

Cairns' coastal humidity and salt air put extra demand on ventilation, waterproofing membranes and fixture material during the wet season. A contractor used to local conditions should specify tapware, exhaust ventilation and wet‑area waterproofing rated for tropical use, not a generic southern‑states build spec. Persistent moisture is also why waterproofing sign‑off and tiling sequencing matter more here than in drier climates, since a poorly sealed membrane shows up faster in Cairns humidity than it would further south. Selecting materials that resist corrosion and mould helps maintain the bathroom's appearance throughout the long rainy period.

Refresh versus full renovation

A refresh keeps the existing layout and updates surfaces, fixtures, vanity, tapware and lighting. Because plumbing and drainage stay in place, it is faster and cheaper than a full renovation, though a licensed plumber must still handle any notifiable plumbing work. A full renovation covers layout changes, plumbing relocation, tiling and fixtures from the studs up, and takes longer because more trades and stages are involved. The decision influences both cost and timeline, so homeowners should weigh the benefits of a modest update against the impact of a complete redesign on their long‑term comfort and property value.

Common questions

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Cairns? Most projects range from about $18,000 for a compact refresh up to $65,000 or more for a full strip-to-stud renovation with custom joinery and premium fixtures, depending on size, layout changes and plumbing work.

How long does a renovation take? A mid‑range job usually takes 4 to 8 weeks. Small refreshes can finish in 2 to 3 weeks, while renovations with custom cabinetry may extend to around 10 weeks.

Do I need a QBCC‑licensed builder? Yes, for any residential work over $3,300 including labour and materials, especially where walls, drainage or waterproofing are affected. A written Domestic Building Contract and QBCC Home Warranty cover are also required.
