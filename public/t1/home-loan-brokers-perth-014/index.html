Home Loan Brokers Perth Explained

Finance Broker Perth presents a Perth broker page where borrowers can speak with a qualified independent broker. Arrive with a borrowing brief, because a first home buyer, refinancing homeowner, investor and construction borrower should not receive the same discussion.

The published page says a broker compares products from 100+ lenders and that there is zero cost to the borrower, with brokers paid by lenders when a loan settles. ASIC Moneysmart guidance on home loans points borrowers toward repayments, fees, loan features and product comparison, so treat the first call as a structured fact find.

  • State the purpose, such as purchase, refinance, investment, construction, commercial finance or vehicle finance.
  • Ask why particular lenders are being considered for your circumstances.
  • Ask how fees, repayments, offset features and lender conditions are compared.
  • Confirm that alternatives are being explained before an application is chosen.

Questions That Reveal The Shortlist Logic

A lender panel is useful only when the borrower can see the filter. Ask what has been ruled in, what has been ruled out, and whether the recommendation is driven by rate, lender policy, loan feature, timing, deposit position or borrower profile.

  • First home buyers: ask how FHBG, deposit size, possible 5% deposit pathways and LMI are being checked.
  • Refinancers: ask whether the review includes your existing lender, loan features, discharge costs and any possible cashback.
  • Investors: ask how interest-only options, equity access and portfolio lending may affect flexibility.
  • Construction borrowers: ask what documents are needed before progress payments are released.
  • Business borrowers: ask whether specialist commercial lenders may be considered if a main bank is unsuitable.

Reading The Zero Cost Statement

The page states there is zero cost to the borrower and says brokers are paid by lenders when the loan settles. That does not answer every cost question. Ask separately about lender fees, product fees, valuation costs, discharge costs and other loan charges.

ASIC Moneysmart encourages borrowers to compare the full cost and features of a home loan, not only the advertised rate. A clear recommendation should explain the trade-off between repayments, features, timing and eligibility in plain language.

Preparing A Cleaner Enquiry

A broker can compare options only against the information provided. Before enquiring, gather income details, existing debts, estimated deposit, current loan balance if refinancing, property use, and contract or build information if available. Investors should also prepare the expected rental position and the reason for considering interest-only or principal and interest repayments.

Turn assumptions into checks. Instead of assuming a government scheme applies, ask the broker to verify it. Instead of assuming a specialist lender is required, ask which mainstream and specialist options would be compared. Instead of asking only for the lowest rate, ask which structure is easier to manage if offset use, future refinancing or repayment changes matter.

  1. Define the loan purpose. Start with whether the enquiry is for purchase, refinance, investment, construction, commercial finance or vehicle finance.
  2. Prepare the core facts. Bring income, debts, deposit or existing loan details, property use and any contract or build information already available.
  3. Ask for comparison logic. Request a plain explanation of which lenders were considered, why options were excluded and what costs or features matter.
  4. Check obligations before applying. Use ASIC Moneysmart guidance to compare repayments, fees and loan features before committing to an application.
