Choosing the right path based on pressure points

  • Deposit size: smaller deposits often require lenders mortgage insurance or a guarantor.
  • Pre approval timing: buyers preparing for an auction need document readiness.
  • Refinance value: loans nearing a fixed rate expiry benefit from a rate review.
  • Income documentation: self employed applicants need thorough tax returns and BAS history.

Common questions

How much deposit do I need to buy in Parramatta? Deposit needs change by lender, property type and whether LMI or a guarantee is part of the structure. It is worth checking the repayment once rates, strata and moving costs are included, rather than looking at deposit size alone.

What is the NSW First Home Owner Grant? The NSW First Home Owner Grant applies to eligible new homes within state caps. It is most useful when tested alongside duty settings, deposit size and the lender's own policy rather than treated as a shortcut on its own.

Can a self-employed borrower still get a home loan? Yes, although lender choice and document quality matter more. Tax returns, BAS history and entity structure all affect which lenders are realistic, and the timing of a purchase relative to a business's financial year can change the outcome.
