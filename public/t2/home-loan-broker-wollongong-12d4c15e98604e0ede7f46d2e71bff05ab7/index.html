Common questions

Is it better to use a mortgage broker or go directly to a bank? A mortgage broker can compare loans from multiple lenders and help you find a product that suits your situation, while a bank will only offer its own loans. In Australia, brokers are required to act in your best interests and explain why a recommended loan is suitable.

Do mortgage brokers in Wollongong charge fees? Most Australian mortgage brokers are paid commission by the lender and do not charge a direct fee to standard home loan customers. However, some brokers may charge a fee for complex lending or specialised advice, and should disclose this clearly up front.

How much deposit do I need to buy? The site does not give one fixed figure, because deposit position is one of the basics that shapes lender policy and is checked before you submit details.
