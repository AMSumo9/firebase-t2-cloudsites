Key Clarifications for a Canberra Broker

A productive initial conversation with a mortgage broker should aim to reduce uncertainty before a credit application is submitted. It's advisable to inquire about the specific lenders being compared, whether specialist lenders are relevant to your situation, what documentation is still outstanding, and if the broker operates under an Australian Credit Licence or as an authorised credit representative.

Deposit and Duty: Enquire whether ACT schemes impact the cash required at settlement. For instance, the ACT Home Buyer Concession Scheme can significantly reduce or eliminate stamp duty for eligible buyers. From July 2025, eligible buyers may pay no stamp duty on properties up to $1,020,000, with concessional rates applying up to $1,455,000, potentially saving up to $35,238. To qualify, buyers must not have owned property in Australia in the past five years and must reside in the property for at least one year.

LMI: Ask about the circumstances under which lenders mortgage insurance may apply and if any guarantee scheme alters this requirement. For example, from October 2025, the First Home Guarantee may allow a 5% deposit without LMI.

Timing: Clarify what steps must be completed before relying on a pre-approval. This includes understanding the conditions for full approval and settlement.

Costs: Understand how lender commissions are disclosed and whether any borrower-paid fees are applicable. The Credit Guide should detail these aspects.

Navigating ACT and National Home Buyer Schemes

Several schemes can assist eligible buyers in the ACT and nationally. The ACT Home Buyer Concession Scheme, the First Home Guarantee, and Help to Buy are key programs to explore. As mentioned, the ACT scheme offers stamp duty concessions for eligible properties. The First Home Guarantee, effective from October 2025, can facilitate a purchase with a 5% deposit without incurring lenders mortgage insurance. The Help to Buy initiative may also permit a 2% deposit. It is important to treat these figures as starting points for verification, as individual eligibility, lender acceptance, and settlement timelines must align for them to practically impact the approval process.

The broker's role is to help navigate these complexities, ensuring that the chosen loan product and any applicable schemes are a suitable fit for the borrower's financial situation and property goals. Verification of eligibility criteria and understanding the specific conditions of each scheme are paramount.

Broker vs. Direct Bank Comparison

Choosing between a mortgage broker and a direct bank depends on your circumstances. A direct bank can be efficient if you already meet their specific credit policies and product offerings. However, a mortgage broker excels in comparison, particularly if you wish to explore options from multiple lenders or have a complex scenario requiring careful structuring before submission.

Brokers typically compare options from over 100 lenders, encompassing major banks, credit unions, and specialist lenders. For standard residential lending, broker services are generally free for borrowers, as lenders pay a commission upon loan settlement. Common questions

What is the role of a mortgage broker in Canberra? A mortgage broker in Canberra acts as an intermediary between borrowers and lenders. They help borrowers find suitable home loan products by comparing options from various financial institutions, assisting with applications, and providing guidance on loan structures and eligibility for schemes.

Are mortgage brokers free for borrowers in Canberra? For standard residential home loans, mortgage broker services are typically free for borrowers. This is because brokers receive a commission from the lender once the loan settles. However, it's always advisable to confirm fee structures and obtain a Credit Guide from the broker.

What documentation is typically required by a mortgage broker? Documentation requirements vary but generally include proof of identity, income verification (payslips, tax returns), details of assets and liabilities, and information about the property you intend to purchase or refinance. For specific schemes, additional supporting documents will be necessary.
