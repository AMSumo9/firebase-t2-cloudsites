When repair and replacement diverge

The source page says replacement is only suggested when repair is not the sensible answer. That is the right question to ask when the door has repeated faults, damaged panels, a fatigued spring setup, or an opener that no longer behaves consistently. A repair may restore operation without replacing the whole door, but a new roller or sectional door can be a cleaner path when the existing system is no longer worth chasing.

For repair work, ask the technician to separate diagnosis, parts and labour in plain language. For replacement, ask what is being supplied, whether a new opener is included, and how the quote treats the existing door and hardware. The published process says the fault is checked and quoted in writing before repair work begins, so the decision should be visible before approval.
