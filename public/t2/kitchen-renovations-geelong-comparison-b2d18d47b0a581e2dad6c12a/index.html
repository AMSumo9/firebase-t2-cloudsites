The first decision is whether to keep the existing footprint or change the layout. A cosmetic refresh focuses on cabinetry, benchtops and splashbacks within the current walls. This is often the most budget-friendly path for Geelong homes where the structure works but finishes are dated. For example, a 1980s galley kitchen in Newtown might only need a cabinet refresh, new benchtops and splashback to look modern again. A full redesign involves moving walls, adding an island bench or opening the kitchen to living areas. This changes the flow and storage but requires more coordination. kitchen renovations geelong experts often recommend 3D visuals before any fabrication begins to test these layout changes. This helps you see how an island bench might fit or how opening up the space affects light and traffic flow before you commit to cutting cabinetry.

Timeline and Planning Phases

Understanding the schedule helps manage expectations and avoid disruption. The design and lead-time planning phase usually takes 2 to 3 months. This includes selecting finishes, finalising layouts and ordering custom items. Once work begins, the on-site renovation period is typically 4 to 8 weeks. However, custom cabinetry has a separate lead time of around 6 weeks, and stone benchtops require a 1 to 3 week template-to-install window. These windows can overlap with some preparation but should appear clearly in your project plan. A standard renovation timeline includes design first, then cabinet fabrication, benchtop templating after cabinets are ready, and finally the fit-off once plumbing and electrical are sequenced. This sequenced approach means you are not chasing multiple trades. VBA/BPC-registered plumbers and Energy Safe Victoria licensed electricians are coordinated behind the scenes to ensure the project runs smoothly.

Cost Drivers and Budgeting

Costs vary significantly based on scope. A cosmetic refresh is listed from around $8,500, depending on finishes and scope. This path keeps the existing footprint and focuses on cabinetry refresh, new benchtops, splashback and appliances. A premium custom fitout starts from $45,000 plus. The strongest cost drivers are custom cabinetry, stone benchtops, splashbacks and layout changes. Stone, laminate and timber benchtop options each sit differently in a budget. Engineered stone is durable and popular, while natural stone offers a unique look but requires more care. Custom cabinetry offers more freedom for pantry pull-outs, tall storage and integrated appliance housings, whereas flat pack kitchens are available where budget is the priority. When comparing quotes, ensure the itemised quote separates design, cabinetry, benchtops, splashbacks, plumbing, electrical coordination and installation. This allows you to compare two proposals on equal terms rather than a single vague total.
