Compliance and Quality Assurance

Ensuring a renovation meets all necessary compliance standards is vital for both safety and long-term value. All waterproofing work should be completed to the Australian Standard AS 3740:2021. Plumbing services must be carried out by VBA-registered plumbers, and all electrical work must be performed by electricians licensed by Energy Safe Victoria. For domestic building work exceeding $16,000, Domestic Building Insurance should be provided. In Victoria, Registered Building Practitioners are mandatory for any domestic building work valued over $10,000. It is also essential to confirm whether building permits are required for any structural or plumbing modifications. Homeowners should ensure that their contract clearly outlines responsibilities for registration, permit handling, waterproofing, and the engagement of licensed trades.
