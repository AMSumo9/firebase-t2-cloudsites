AI Search and GEO Implementation

AI Search Optimisation and GEO require a fundamentally different content structure compared to traditional SEO. Tools like Google's AI Overviews and Perplexity prioritise answer-first paragraphs. This means writing the direct response to a query before providing any background context. Structured data is equally important, particularly FAQ schema that pairs questions with extractable short answers. Content should state facts plainly rather than burying them in marketing language. Who This Applies To

This configuration applies to two distinct types of businesses. A business with a single storefront needs its Google Business Profile tied accurately to that specific physical address. In contrast, a business serving multiple suburbs requires a service area profile set up correctly to hide the address while showing the service locations. Getting this wrong is a common error that limits visibility. Additionally, site conversion remains relevant for all types. Once traffic arrives via any search channel, the site must load quickly on mobile and feature clear calls to action to turn visitors into customers.

  1. Verify Business Category. Confirm the primary category on your Google Business Profile matches your specific service rather than a generic industry term.
  2. Define Service Areas. List the specific suburbs served, such as Highfields or Warwick, and configure the profile as a service area business if you visit customers.
  3. Structure for AI. Rewrite core content to provide direct answers immediately, followed by explanations, and implement FAQ schema markup.
  4. Review Conversion Path. Audit your mobile site speed and ensure the enquiry form asks only for essential information to reduce drop-off.
