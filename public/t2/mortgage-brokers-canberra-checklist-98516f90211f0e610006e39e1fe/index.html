Key Questions for a Canberra Broker

A productive initial conversation with a mortgage broker should aim to clarify uncertainties before a credit application is submitted. Essential questions include:

  • Which lenders are being considered, and are specialist lenders relevant to your situation?
  • What documentation is still required to proceed with the application?
  • Is the broker operating under an Australian Credit Licence or as an authorised credit representative? The broker must provide a Credit Guide before offering credit assistance.
  • How might ACT schemes impact the cash required at settlement, particularly regarding deposit and stamp duty?
  • When might lenders mortgage insurance apply, and could a guarantee scheme alter this requirement?
  • What steps must be completed before pre-approval can be relied upon?
  • How is the lender's commission disclosed, and are there any borrower fees involved?

Navigating ACT Schemes

For eligible buyers in the ACT, several schemes can significantly impact the home-buying process. These include the ACT Home Buyer Concession Scheme, the First Home Guarantee, and Help to Buy. From July 2025, eligible buyers may pay no stamp duty on properties up to $1,020,000, with concessional rates applying to properties up to $1,455,000, potentially saving up to $35,238. To qualify, buyers must not have owned property in Australia in the preceding five years and must reside in the property for at least one year.

Additionally, from October 2025, the First Home Guarantee may allow a 5% deposit without requiring lenders mortgage insurance. The Help to Buy scheme could potentially enable a 2% deposit. It is crucial to treat these figures as prompts for verification during your advice session, as eligibility criteria, lender acceptance, and settlement timing must align to practically affect your approval path.

Broker vs. Direct Bank Comparison

Choosing between a mortgage broker and a direct bank depends on your circumstances. A direct bank can be efficient if you already meet their specific credit policies and product offerings. However, a mortgage broker excels in comparison, particularly if you wish to explore options from multiple lenders or have a complex scenario requiring careful structuring before submission.

Brokers typically compare options across more than 100 lenders, encompassing major banks, credit unions, and specialist lenders. For standard residential lending, broker services are generally free for borrowers, as lenders provide commission upon loan settlement.
