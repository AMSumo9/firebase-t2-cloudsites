Comparing Quotes

Comparing quotes requires asking the same four questions of every provider to ensure a fair comparison.

  • Ask about demolition to see if disposal is included or billed separately.
  • Check benchtop details to confirm if the allowance is a named stone product or a figure that can move.
  • Inquire about older homes to understand how unknowns behind existing cabinetry are handled.
  • Confirm strata approval to ensure body corporate steps are included in the timeline and price.

These details often explain price differences more than the headline figure. A published cost band is only useful as a comparison frame, not a promise for your job. When two quotes land at different points in that range, the gap is usually scope, not markup. Asking the same four questions of every quote turns a headline number into a real comparison.

Timeline and Process

The project timeline begins with a one business day response to your initial brief. Once a provider is selected, the build phase usually takes four to eight weeks from contract signing to handover. Active site work, such as demolition and installation, typically occupies two to three weeks once cabinetry is on site. The gap between signing and site work is often driven by lead times for cabinetry, stone and appliances. Stone benchtops, in particular, often require weeks of curing and fabrication time before installation. For strata projects, factor in additional time for body corporate approval processes and access scheduling. The stated sequence is straightforward: submit the project brief, get a response within one business day, then move to quote and build.

Cost Considerations

Published cost bands for Western Sydney kitchen work provide a useful starting point for budgeting. western sydney kitchen costs generally fall between $25,000 and $65,000 for a fully fitted renovation. A budget refresh, which might involve cabinet door changes and splashback updates, generally starts around $18,000. Mid-range full renovations typically fall between $35,000 and $50,000. Fully fitted renovations with stone, brass and custom joinery often exceed $80,000. These figures are indicative and subject to individual circumstances. When comparing quotes, look at the written inclusions rather than the headline price. A lower quote might exclude demolition or use a lower-grade stone. A higher quote might include premium appliances or a more extensive joinery scope. It is vital to ask each quoter the same set of questions so the answers are comparable.

  1. Share the brief. Tell the service the suburb, rough scope and main kitchen changes you want.
  2. Ask comparison questions. Put the same demolition, benchtop, older-home and strata questions to every quote.
  3. Check before signing. Confirm licence, insurance, warranty, contract detail and any approval step in writing.
