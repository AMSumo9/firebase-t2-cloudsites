The published details from Bathroom Renovation Camberwell are most useful as a scoping reference. The page separates full bathroom renovations, ensuite work, small bathroom solutions, luxury upgrades, waterproofing and heritage sensitive work. That helps a homeowner describe the job before asking for prices.

A full strip out and rebuild is not the same task as improving storage or replacing selected fixtures. The published inclusions for a complete renovation are waterproofing, plumbing, tiling, fixtures, vanities and finishes. If structural or plumbing work needs permits, the page says those permits are arranged. Ask for those inclusions to be written into the quote.

  • Confirm whether the job is a full renovation, ensuite, small room, luxury upgrade or period home bathroom.
  • Ask which trades handle plumbing, electrical, waterproofing, tiling and cabinetry.
  • Check permits, contracts, warranties and insurance before work starts.

Timing and Trade Sequencing

The page says standard bathroom construction typically takes 2 to 4 weeks. Planning and permit approvals, if required, may add 2 to 6 weeks. It also states that small bathrooms may complete in 2 to 3 weeks, while luxury renovations with structural changes can take 6 to 8 weeks.

Ask for a stage by stage schedule before demolition. The order should show when selections are due, when plumbing and electrical work occur, when waterproofing is completed, when tiling starts and when fixtures are installed. If approval is needed before structural or plumbing work, the schedule should show that step before the room is stripped out.

Compliance and Warranty Questions

The page states that waterproofing is completed to AS 3740:2021, plumbing is handled by VBA registered plumbers, electrical work by Energy Safe Victoria licensed electricians, and Domestic Building Insurance is provided for work over $16,000. These checks belong in the quote discussion because much of a bathroom renovation is hidden once finishes are installed.

Ask who the Registered Building Practitioner is, which licensed trades will attend, and how waterproofing will be documented before tiling. The page also refers to workmanship warranties, waterproofing warranties required by law, and manufacturer warranties on fixtures and fittings. The quote should make each warranty clear.

What to Ask Before Signing

A useful quote describes the room, inclusions, exclusions, finish level, trade responsibilities, expected timing, permit assumptions and payment milestones. Prepare a brief with current problems, preferred fixtures, storage needs, must keep features and the level of finish you expect.

For a fixed price quote, ask what fixtures are allowed for, what happens if higher priced tapware is chosen, whether custom joinery is included, and whether demolition, disposal, permits and final handover are covered. Compare providers against the same written scope, not a headline price.

  1. Define the room. Write down whether the project is a compact bathroom, ensuite, full renovation, luxury upgrade or period sensitive design.
  2. Set inclusions. Ask for waterproofing, plumbing, electrical, tiling, fixtures, vanity, permits and warranties to be shown in writing.
  3. Check timing. Compare the proposed construction schedule with any planning or permit period that may apply.
  4. Confirm compliance roles. Identify the registered practitioner and the licensed plumbers and electricians before work starts.
  5. Compare like for like. Assess quotes against the same scope, finish level and exclusions rather than headline price alone.
