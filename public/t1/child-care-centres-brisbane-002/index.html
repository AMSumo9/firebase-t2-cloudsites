What to compare on each profile

Once you open a provider profile, the most useful checks are the ones the directory already surfaces. Read the reviews in full rather than stopping at the score. Look at the photos to see whether they answer the questions you still have about the service presentation. Compare the listed services and pricing in the same order every time so one strong profile does not distract you from missing detail elsewhere.

The page also highlights verified businesses, which is helpful when you want a cleaner shortlist, but verification should not replace your own comparison notes. A practical worksheet can stay simple.

  • Reviews: what parents keep mentioning, positively or negatively.
  • Photos: whether the images help you understand the setting.
  • Services: whether the listing matches the type of care you need.
  • Pricing: whether the provider gives enough detail for a meaningful next step.

Turn the shortlist into useful enquiries

The contact stage is where a long list either becomes manageable or turns messy. The published process is straightforward, call, email, or request a quote through the form. The part worth adding is discipline. Send the same core questions to each provider in the same care category, then keep the replies together so you are comparing like with like rather than whichever response arrives first.

That is especially useful when a Brisbane search starts broad and then narrows. If one listing gives strong photos but limited pricing detail, and another has clearer pricing but fewer reviews, your next action is not to guess. Use direct contact to fill the same information gaps across the shortlist.
