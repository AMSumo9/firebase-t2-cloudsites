Separate visibility, traffic and conversion

Organic visibility, paid traffic and website conversion are connected but distinct decisions. A search optimisation brief should identify which service pages or local searches matter. A paid advertising brief should identify the campaign, offer and landing page to be reviewed. A website brief should explain what visitors need to understand before making contact.

Use these questions when comparing proposals:

  • Which page, profile or campaign is included?
  • What change will be made first?
  • What evidence will show that the work was completed?
  • When will the work be reviewed?
  • What decision should the review support?

This approach prevents a general activity list from being mistaken for a defined project. It also shows whether several services support one customer journey or simply sit beside one another.
