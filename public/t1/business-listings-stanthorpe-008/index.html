How to read reviews without overvaluing one number

The featured listings show review counts and ratings where available, including accommodation and cafe examples. Logger's Rest B&B is shown with a 4.9 rating from 123 reviews, Artworks Granite Belt with a 5.0 rating from 26 reviews, Tiny Away Escape @ Granite Belt with a 4.5 Google rating from 272 reviews at the time of review, and Murray Gardens Cottages & Motel with a 3.5 rating from 163 reviews.

Those figures are useful starting points, not a complete decision. A higher rating with fewer reviews can mean something different from a lower rating with a larger sample. The directory's stronger use case is comparison: look at rating, count, photos, service description and whether the business category matches the job.

  • For accommodation, compare the type of stay described, not just the score.
  • For trades, check whether the listing names the kind of work you need.
  • For cafes and restaurants, read the setting and offer as well as the review summary.
  • For quote-based work, use the form or direct contact after you have a shortlist.

Category choice should follow the job, not the nearest label

A local directory is most useful when the category chosen matches the actual outcome needed. A renovation question may begin with builders, but a smaller issue might need a painter, plumber, electrician or landscaper instead. The source page encourages browsing categories or searching for the trade needed in Stanthorpe, which keeps the first step flexible.

For home services, prepare a short job note before contacting anyone. Include the property type, the visible issue, whether it is repair, installation or comparison shopping, and whether photos would help explain the request. The directory states that users can compare services and pricing, so a clear first enquiry makes any later comparison easier.

For visitor or lifestyle decisions, the same logic applies. Accommodation listings should be read for the kind of stay described, while cafes, restaurants and wineries should be compared on what the listing says they offer. The aim is not to contact every result. It is to identify the few listings whose descriptions answer the actual need.
