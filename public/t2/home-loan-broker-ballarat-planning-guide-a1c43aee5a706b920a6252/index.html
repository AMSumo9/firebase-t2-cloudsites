Match the questions to the borrower type

The published service range covers first home buying, refinancing, investment property, construction, self employed files and guarantor support, so the first meeting should not use one generic checklist. A first home buyer needs the boundaries explained: which parts depend on lender policy and which rely on separate eligibility rules. The source material cites the First Home Guarantee, a 5 percent deposit and no LMI for eligible borrowers, plus stamp duty concessions and the First Home Super Saver Scheme.

For refinancing, the sharper test is whether the new structure improves cash flow or flexibility once fees and features are included. For investors, the conversation should cover tax effective structuring, interest only options and lenders comfortable with multiple properties. For construction, progress draw facilities tied to milestones mean funding release timing and stage documents become part of approval planning.

Trust signals should become specific trade offs

The cited protections matter because individual brokers operate under an Australian Credit Licence, are regulated by ASIC, hold AFCA membership and maintain professional indemnity insurance. Those points should still be converted into plain English for your file. Ask why one lender and one structure were recommended over the alternatives reviewed.
