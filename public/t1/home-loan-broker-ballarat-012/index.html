Documents To Prepare

The published process starts with a free chat about goals, then comparison across 40+ lenders, then borrower selection and settlement support. That process is easier when the file is organised before product comparison begins.

Prepare income evidence, existing loan statements, living expense details, savings history and property documents where available. For a refinance, add the current loan balance, repayments, offset or redraw use and any reason you want equity access, renovations funding or debt consolidation. For a build, separate the land position, build contract, progress payment schedule and whether the project is an own-land build, house and land package or knock-down rebuild.

For a guarantor structure, ask what obligation the guarantor accepts, how the guarantee is limited, and what would need to happen before release is considered. Do not treat family help as a substitute for understanding the lender conditions.

Compliance And Choice Checks

The page states that brokers in the pathway operate under an Australian Credit Licence arrangement, are regulated by ASIC, hold AFCA membership for dispute resolution and maintain professional indemnity insurance. It also refers to the Best Interests Duty, which has applied to mortgage brokers since January 2021.

Ask for the credit representative details, licence holder, complaints process and commission disclosure. Then compare the recommendation on rate, repayment, fees, features, flexibility, approval conditions and settlement risk.

A lower rate is not enough if the loan removes a feature you need or adds a condition you cannot satisfy before finance approval. Keep a written shortlist showing lender, reason for recommendation, required documents, expected approval path and borrower actions.

  1. Define the loan purpose. State whether the loan is for a first purchase, refinance, investment, construction, self-employed application or guarantor structure.
  2. Prepare evidence. Collect income records, existing loan statements, expense details, savings history and property or build documents before lender comparison.
  3. Compare the shortlist. Ask why each lender was included, what was excluded, and which conditions could affect approval or settlement.
  4. Confirm compliance details. Check credit licence arrangements, commission disclosure, complaints process and how the Best Interests Duty is applied.
  5. Choose with settlement in mind. Select the loan that balances rate, features, conditions, document readiness and settlement timing.
