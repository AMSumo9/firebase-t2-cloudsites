Timeline breakdown

Understanding the timeline helps manage expectations. A standard full renovation usually runs around 6 to 12 weeks from contract to handover. This timeline is split into fabrication and on-site work. Cabinetry and benchtop fabrication often takes 4 to 6 weeks, then the on-site stage usually takes 2 to 4 weeks for demolition, install, plumbing, electrical and finishing. A makeover is generally quicker. The biggest delays are not always on site. The more practical risk is starting fabrication before layout, materials and appliance choices are settled. To keep the program on track, ask when selections must be locked in and when templates are taken for benchtops.

The business outlines a clear four-stage process. First, a free measure-up visits your home to measure the space and talk through how you use the kitchen. Second, design and quote involves planning the layout, materials and lighting, showing drawings and providing an itemised fixed-price written quote. Third, build and install involves stripping out the old kitchen and installing cabinetry, benchtops, plumbing, electrical, splashback and appliances. Finally, handover finishes the job, cleans up and hands over a working kitchen with warranty and compliance paperwork in writing. Planning the program around fabrication, not just site days, is essential for staying on schedule.
