Where the money moves

With a 30+ lender panel described as major banks, non-banks and specialist equipment financiers, the practical task is like-for-like matching. A cheaper-sounding quote can change once term, balloon, deposit, asset age and security are aligned. For equipment finance, the equipment commonly acts as security, with extra security rarely sought on standard applications. For broader business lending, the stated range runs from $10,000 to $5 million, while working capital starts at $20,000 and reaches $500,000+. Those bands frame the first call; they are not a promise that every profile will clear the same hurdle.

GST, ownership and deductions

Tax timing can alter the real cost even when the headline fee is nil. Under a chattel mortgage, the borrower owns the asset from day one, may claim GST upfront and may benefit from depreciation deductions. Under a finance lease, the lender owns the asset, GST is charged on repayments, and the end choice can be return or purchase. Hire purchase sits in the same family of questions, especially where usage, mileage, obsolescence and balance-sheet preference matter. The prudent step is to test each structure with an accountant before signing, because GST, depreciation and cash-flow treatment can move the after-tax answer more than a small rate gap.

Term, balloon and asset life

Commercial vehicle finance is stated as offering flexible terms up to 7 years, but a longer term is not automatically cheaper in substance. A balloon can reduce regular repayments and free short-term cash, then leave a final payment that must be funded by sale, refinance, trade-in or retained profit. Match the term to asset life: a truck worked hard over five years should not sit on a structure that assumes seven unless the numbers still work at disposal. Seasonal operators should also ask how repayment timing aligns with harvest, contract milestones or debtor cycles.

A fee-aware comparison routine

Bring the asset, amount, timing, deposit position, trading history and repayment preference into one short brief. State whether the item is new or used, the expected term, any balloon preference, and how the asset supports revenue or operations. Then compare quotes on total cost, approval conditions, payout flexibility, security, covenants and end-of-term value. Published timing is 24 hours for standard pre-approval, 2 to 5 business days for full approval, and possible same-day outcomes for smaller unsecured loans. Common questions

Does $0 broker fees mean borrowing is cost-free? No. It means the stated broker charge is nil. Interest, lender fees where charged, balloons, security and tax timing can still affect the total cost, so compare total repayments and conditions rather than the fee line alone.

Is 24 hour approval guaranteed? The stated timing is pre-approval often within 24 hours for standard applications, with full approval typically 2 to 5 business days. Smaller unsecured loans may be faster, while complexity, documents and asset type can extend assessment.

What should be prepared before asking for a quote? Prepare identification, business details, asset quotes or invoices, basic financial information, and notes on seasonal trading or uneven cash flow. Clear purpose, amount, deposit position and repayment preference help produce comparable quotes.
