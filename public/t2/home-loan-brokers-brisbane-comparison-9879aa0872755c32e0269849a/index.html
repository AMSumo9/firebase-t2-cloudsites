Compare process quality

Once you have a shortlist, compare process quality. The directory allows you to view photos, compare services and pricing, and read reviews. Reviews are useful for spotting consistency, but do not replace your own questioning. Look for brokers who explain fees, features and trade-offs in plain language. They should identify questions you should resolve before an application is lodged. This is especially important when considering features like an offset account or interest-only loans, which can significantly affect your repayments. A broker who rushes past these distinctions and another who works through them carefully will offer a very different experience. You want a partner who helps you understand the trade-offs, not just the headline rate.

For investors, Moneysmart frames property as an investment that should be understood on its own terms, so ask how the broker compares borrowing options without assuming the property decision is automatically sound.

Questions to ask before applying

Before requesting a quote, prepare a short set of questions. Moneysmart advises borrowers to compare options and ask brokers the right questions. Ask which category best fits you right now, first home buyer, refinance, investment, or construction. Ask what information they need before comparing loan options properly. Ask what features are worth discussing for your situation, and which ones may add cost without much benefit. If you are considering switching, what costs or break-even questions should you examine first? The strongest responses are usually specific about inputs, process and trade-offs. Vague answers can still be useful, but specific ones indicate a broker who understands your needs and can provide tailored advice.
