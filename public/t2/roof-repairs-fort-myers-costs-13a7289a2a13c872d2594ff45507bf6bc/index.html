Repair versus replacement

A repair is usually the cleaner option when the problem is localised and the rest of the roof still has service life left. The source material specifically lists leaks, missing shingles, cracked tiles and worn flashing as repairable situations, provided the inspection supports that narrower scope. Material type changes what a repair looks like. An asphalt shingle roof can need attention to storm exposure and ventilation. Concrete and clay tile roofs may involve tile repair or re-bedding. Metal jobs can centre on panels and wind performance. Low-slope areas over additions or lanais often need membrane thinking rather than shingle thinking, with TPO, EPDM or modified bitumen named on the source page.

Replacement moves into view when the roof is ageing, when storm damage is broad, or when the roof covering and the layers beneath it need work from the decking and underlayment upward. The source page describes replacement as a full tear-off and re-roof for shingle, tile and metal homes across Fort Myers and Lee County. That fuller scope is also where written detail matters most. The page says the matched contractor puts scope, price and terms in writing before work starts, and that any earlier number is only a guide. That protects you from comparing a partial patch quote against a complete re-roof scope that includes different components.

Storm damage and insurance

After a storm, the source page highlights fast temporary tarping and leak control to limit water entry, followed by a documented assessment for permanent repair and any homeowner insurance claim. That sequence is useful because it separates the urgent task, keeping more water out, from the slower task of deciding the lasting fix. For the homeowner, the value is in the documentation as much as the tarp. Ask what was observed after the storm, what areas were made temporarily safe, and what still needs a permanent scope. If the roof also needs an inspection for the insurer, the source page says inspections can support a wind mitigation report. This is also the point to keep all price talk provisional until the roof has been walked and the written estimate is issued.
