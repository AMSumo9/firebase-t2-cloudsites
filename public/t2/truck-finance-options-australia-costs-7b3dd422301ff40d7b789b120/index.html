Common Truck Finance Options

The most common truck finance options Australia offers include chattel mortgages, hire purchase agreements, and novated leases. A chattel mortgage allows you to own the truck outright at the end of the loan term, often with tax benefits related to depreciation. This structure is popular for business owners as it provides clear ownership and potential tax deductions. Hire purchase is similar but may have higher interest rates and less flexibility regarding early repayment. Novated leases are ideal for owner-drivers as they allow salary packaging, effectively using pre-tax income to cover the lease payments. Selecting the right option depends on your business structure and tax situation, so consulting with a mortgage broker is highly recommended.

Key Factors Affecting Your Rate

Several factors influence the interest rates you receive for truck finance. The loan-to-value ratio is a primary determinant, with lenders typically preferring a lower ratio to mitigate risk. A higher ratio, such as 90% or more, often triggers lenders mortgage insurance, increasing the cost of borrowing. Your credit history also plays a significant role, as a strong credit score can unlock better rates. Additionally, the age and condition of the truck can affect the loan terms, with newer assets generally securing better financing deals. A mortgage broker can assess these factors to present the best possible options from a wide range of lenders, ensuring you do not overpay for your truck finance.

Common questions

What is the deposit for a truck loan? A standard deposit for truck finance is typically 20% of the vehicle's value, though this can vary depending on the lender and the loan-to-value ratio.

Can I refinance my truck loan? Yes, refinancing is a common strategy to lower interest rates or change loan terms if your financial situation has improved.

What is Lenders Mortgage Insurance? Lenders Mortgage Insurance is a fee charged by lenders when you borrow more than 80% of the asset's value to protect them against default.
