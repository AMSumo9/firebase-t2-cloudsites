Why a licensed electrician is required

When a powered opener's wiring becomes damaged or needs adjustment, the work crosses into electrical safety territory. Queensland law under the Electrical Safety Act mandates that only a licensed electrician can handle hardwired connections. This protects homeowners from unsafe DIY attempts and ensures compliance with state regulations. Common DIY signs to watch for

  • A door that drops fast or feels heavy often signals a broken spring and should not be adjusted by hand.
  • Grinding or squealing noises during travel usually indicate roller or track wear, not just a lubrication issue.
  • If the door reverses or stops partway, a misaligned safety beam or track obstruction may be present.
  • Uneven lifting, where one side rises faster, points to spring or cable imbalance.
  • Remote or keypad sync failures suggest programming issues rather than mechanical faults.
