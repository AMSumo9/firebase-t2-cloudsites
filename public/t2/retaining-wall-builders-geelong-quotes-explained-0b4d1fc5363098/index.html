Permit, Boundary And Easement Checks

In Victoria, a building permit is required for a retaining wall 1 metre or more high. It also notes that a lower wall may still require one when it is associated with other building work or protects adjoining property. A registered building surveyor should assess the site rather than relying on a height rule alone. Planning controls are a separate question. Overlays, zoning, easements and title restrictions are property-specific checks that can affect the project before work begins. That is especially relevant where a wall alignment looks obvious on the ground but may cross an easement or sit close to services. Ask whether the wall height and purpose create a building permit question. Ask whether any planning overlay, title restriction or easement affects the alignment. Ask how excavation or retention near a boundary will be reviewed before digging. Ask who will separate approval costs, engineering inputs and construction exclusions in writing.

Drainage Belongs In The Quote

Drainage is not a finishing detail. Subsoil pipe, filter fabric, aggregate, surface grading and lawful stormwater disposal are coordinated parts of a retaining wall drainage discussion.
