Matching the Repair to the Roof System

The source page highlights that not every roof is the same job. Asphalt shingle is the most common local roof type, requiring attention to ventilation due to Florida heat. Barrel tile roofs are common on stucco and Mediterranean style homes, often needing re-bedding or replacement rather than a simple patch. Standing seam metal offers longer service life and strong wind performance. Low slope sections, such as lanais, should be assessed as membrane work using TPO or EPDM systems.

Ensure the contractor understands the specific system before agreeing to a scope. Missing shingles and worn flashing point to targeted repair questions. Cracked tiles need a tile specific scope, not a generic patch allowance. Lanai and low slope areas should be priced as membrane sections. This ensures the written quote reflects the actual work required.
