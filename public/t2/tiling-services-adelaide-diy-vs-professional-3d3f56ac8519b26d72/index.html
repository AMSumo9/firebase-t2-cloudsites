Checks Before Accepting a Scope

When evaluating Adelaide tiling services, compare the detailed written scope rather than relying on brief descriptions. A robust scope clearly defines the actual surface, anticipated water exposure, assumptions regarding preparation, waterproofing or screeding, and specific finish details. If any item appears to be missing, inquire whether it is included, excluded, or subject to further inspection. For wet areas, confirm that membrane boundaries, penetrations, threshold heights, curing sequences, and handover procedures have been adequately addressed. Curing times should align with the membrane system's specifications, film thickness, and site conditions, rather than a generalised rule. For visible tiling work, clarify how tile size, trims, grout selection, movement joints, and transitions to adjacent flooring will be managed. For exterior surfaces, include questions about drainage, required slip resistance, and environmental exposure.
