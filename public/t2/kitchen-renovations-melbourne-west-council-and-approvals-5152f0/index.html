Victorian contract and insurance checkpoints

Victoria's Domestic Building Contracts Act 1995 sets a written major domestic building contract expectation once domestic building work exceeds $10,000. Public guidance in June 2026 often described domestic building insurance as relevant above $16,000, with Home Warranty Insurance reform flagged from 1 July 2026. Verify the current regulator position before signing because thresholds and insurance names can change.

Ask for scope, price, payment stages and variation handling in writing. The renovation flow is four stages: free measure-up, design and quote, build and install, then handover. Materials with compliance consequences

From 1 July 2024, engineered stone containing at least 1% crystalline silica cannot be manufactured, supplied, processed or installed in Australia. Current options discussed with homeowners include low-silica compliant surfaces, porcelain, sintered stone, natural stone, laminate, stainless steel and solid timber.

Material choice is practical as well as visual. Confirm templating, assumed cabinetry tolerances, splashback interfaces, appliance cut-outs and the date final selections must be locked. For a period kitchen, record how new joinery will meet older openings without stripping away character.

A pre-fabrication approval checklist

  1. Define whether the job is cosmetic, structural, service-heavy or character-sensitive.
  2. Book the measure-up with usage notes, pain points and appliances that stay or move.
  3. Review drawings before cabinetry production, including lighting and clearance assumptions.
  4. Check the written quote for itemised scope, fixed price, payment stages, permits, trade responsibilities and variations.
  5. Confirm which warranty and compliance documents arrive at handover.

Common questions

Does every kitchen need council approval? Not always. The practical test is whether the project changes structure, services, drainage, ventilation or protected character. A cosmetic refresh can still involve licensed plumbing or electrical work, so ask what certification is included even when no planning pathway is expected.

When should approvals be settled? Before cabinetry and benchtop fabrication. A 6 to 12 week full renovation usually contains 4 to 6 weeks of fabrication and 2 to 4 weeks on site, so late approval questions can compress the build stage.

Which benchtops are relevant after 2024? Avoid banned high-silica engineered stone. Discuss compliant low-silica products, porcelain, sintered stone, natural stone, laminate, stainless steel or solid timber, matched to use, templating and installation requirements.
