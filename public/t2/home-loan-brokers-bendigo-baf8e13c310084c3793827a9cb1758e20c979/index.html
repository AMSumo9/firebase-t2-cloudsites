First Home Buyer Considerations

Brokers can assist eligible buyers in understanding the Victorian First Home Owner Grant for new home purchases and the federal First Home Guarantee, managed by Housing Australia. For eligible first-time buyers, it may be possible to purchase a property with a deposit as low as 5% without requiring LMI, provided the property price falls within specified caps. It is important not to assume automatic eligibility for these schemes. Prospective buyers should be prepared to provide details about their income, savings history, existing financial commitments, intended purchase location, whether the property is new or established, and their preferred settlement timeframe. Resources like ASIC's Moneysmart offer further guidance on home loan features and associated costs, encouraging borrowers to seek explanations of repayment options, loan features, and potential trade-offs, rather than focusing solely on the lowest advertised interest rate.

Understanding Loan Approval Timelines

The guide provides two practical timelines: pre-approval, often referred to as conditional approval, is typically valid for approximately 90 days, though this can vary by lender. The subsequent stage, from formal application to loan settlement, generally takes about four to six weeks, again depending on the lender and individual circumstances. These figures should be used as planning estimates rather than firm guarantees. Pre-approval indicates a lender's potential borrowing amount before a property is found. Final approval is contingent upon the submission of all required documentation, the lender's assessment, and the specifics of the chosen property. Borrowers should clarify what factors might alter the loan offer between conditional and formal approval, which documents may have expiry dates, and whether the anticipated settlement date aligns with the lender's typical processing times.

Broker Standards and Disclosure Requirements

In Australia, residential mortgage brokers are typically compensated through commissions paid by lenders, rather than directly by the borrower. Any exceptions to this arrangement must be disclosed upfront, in accordance with ASIC regulations. Brokers operating under an Australian Credit Licence or as Credit Representatives are bound by specific conduct obligations. Crucially, Australian mortgage brokers are legally required to act in the consumer's best interests when providing credit assistance. Borrowers should inquire about the broker's remuneration structure, the range of lenders they work with, any applicable fees, how recommendations are documented, and the process for handling disputes. The guide also mentions access to AFCA for external dispute resolution. It is recommended to retain all credit guides, loan comparisons, and written recommendations provided by the broker for future reference.

Common questions

How much deposit do I need for a home loan in Bendigo? For eligible first home buyers, it may be possible to purchase with as little as a 5% deposit without Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI) under schemes like the federal First Home Guarantee, subject to property price caps and eligibility criteria. Generally, a larger deposit can lead to better loan terms.

How long does home loan pre-approval last? Pre-approval, also known as conditional approval, is typically valid for around 90 days. This timeframe can vary depending on the specific lender's policies and your circumstances. It is important to confirm the expiry date with your lender.

What is the typical time from application to settlement for a home loan? The period from formal loan application to settlement usually takes approximately four to six weeks. This duration is influenced by the lender's processing times, the complexity of your application, and the availability of all required documentation and property valuations.
