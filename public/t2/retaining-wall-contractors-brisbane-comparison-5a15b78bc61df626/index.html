Practical steps to get accurate quotes

Follow these steps to ensure every contractor prices the same job:

  • Define the wall type and scope in a single brief, including site notes and photos.
  • Choose the nearest category, repairs, a specific wall type, or the broader contractor category.
  • Send the same brief to each provider via the same channel (form, email, or call).
  • Read reviews and inspect project photos to confirm the contractor's experience matches your brief.
  • Compare the responses, focusing on scope rather than guesswork, and select the contractor whose quote aligns with your project requirements.

  3. Review profiles. Check reviews, photos, and service descriptions to confirm the contractor's experience matches your brief.

Common questions

What categories should I start with if I am unsure of the wall type? Begin with the general contractor category, then examine each profile to see if retaining wall work is clearly described. If the material is known, jump straight to that specific wall type category.

How do I ensure fair comparisons between contractors? Use the same brief, photos, and site notes for every enquiry. This way differences in quotes reflect scope rather than vague descriptions.
