The borrowing audience described for Bendigo and regional Victoria is commercial, not residential. Owners in agriculture, manufacturing, trades, professional services and construction fit the frame, along with purchases such as medical equipment, office fitouts, technology, trucks, utes, vans, trailers and industrial machinery. The common thread is that the funded item or the cash flow need is tied to how the business earns.

A household looking for a standard home loan sits outside this scope. That distinction matters because eligibility questions for a business file revolve around trading activity, the asset or cash gap, and repayment shape, not owner-occupier lending criteria.

The three tests behind most eligibility calls

Behind the lender panel comparison, most eligibility decisions reduce to three tests:

  • Purpose test: the money has a defined job, such as a named asset, a machinery upgrade, a vehicle replacement or a specific expansion cost. A vague request is harder to place than a described one.
  • Security test: the lender asks whether the equipment or vehicle itself will stand as security, or whether the request will sit unsecured. Asset-backed files are generally faster to assess than commercial property-backed ones.
  • Repayment test: the term, any balloon payment and the expected life of the asset must line up with how the business actually earns. Seasonal operators, for example, should raise repayment timing against revenue cycles such as harvest or contracting periods.

How eligibility shifts between funding types

Eligibility is not one standard. The published guide notes that equipment finance is generally faster than commercial property lending because the asset can serve as security, and that additional security is rarely required for standard equipment applications. Commercial vehicle funding covers new and used trucks, utes, vans and trailers with flexible terms up to 7 years. Working capital options span cash flow solutions, debtor finance and overdraft alternatives from $20,000 to $500,000+, where the question is whether the need is temporary liquidity or a longer-term structure.

A smaller unsecured facility may suit a short cash flow problem, while asset-backed finance tends to suit a defined purchase with a clear useful life. Choosing the quickest path before choosing the right one is a common eligibility mistake.

The document set that moves an enquiry forward

The details that decide timing are concrete. Before seeking options, a borrower should be able to state the asset description, the purchase price or funding amount, whether the asset is new or used, the business purpose, the preferred term, whether a balloon payment is on the table, and basic trading information. For working capital, the stronger file describes the cash flow gap, debtor timing or the overdraft alternative under consideration, rather than simply asking for the largest limit available.

It is also reasonable to ask which lenders are in the comparison, why some are excluded, whether the asset itself is expected to serve as security, and what settlement documents are needed before a supplier deadline is locked in. Licensing signals belong in the same conversation: network partners are described as holding Australian Credit Licences, maintaining AFCA membership as required by law, and carrying professional indemnity insurance.

Checking your position before you lodge

Eligibility is best treated as something to test before the application, not something to discover during it. A practical sequence is to name the job of the money, gather the asset or cash flow detail above, decide whether a secured or unsecured stance fits, and note any seasonal or supplier timing that affects the term.
