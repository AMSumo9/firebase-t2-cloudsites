Types of Truck Finance Available

There are several types of finance products available for trucks. New Truck Loans are designed for brand new vehicles, often offering lower interest rates due to the vehicle's higher resale value and lower risk to the lender. Used Truck Loans are available for pre-owned vehicles, which may have different depreciation rates and insurance costs, but can be a more cost-effective entry point.

Asset Finance is a broad category that covers the purchase of trucks and other business assets. This can include Equipment Finance for specialised machinery attached to trucks or used in conjunction with them. Additionally, Business Loans may be structured to cover the purchase of a fleet of trucks rather than a single vehicle. Other finance types like Green Loans and Luxury Car Loans demonstrate the range of products available for different asset classes.

Key Factors to Compare

When comparing truck finance options, you should evaluate the Loan-to-Value Ratio (LVR). This ratio compares the amount you are borrowing to the value of the truck. A higher LVR often requires a larger deposit and may attract Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI), which protects the lender if you default.

Interest rates can be variable or fixed. A variable rate may fluctuate with the market, influenced by the Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA), while a fixed rate remains constant for a set period. You should also consider the Interest-only loan option, which allows you to pay only the interest for a set time, reducing your monthly repayments while you build equity. Furthermore, understanding concepts like Negative gearing can be beneficial for business owners looking to offset costs against income.
