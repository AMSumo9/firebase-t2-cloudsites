The comparison is less about brand names and more about the component that has failed. A roller, sectional or tilt door may share symptoms, yet the repair path differs once the technician checks springs, cables, rollers, tracks, panels, remotes, keypads or the opener. State the door type first, then describe whether the door is stuck open, stuck shut, uneven or unsecured.

  • Mechanical parts: springs, lifting cables, track rollers and damaged panels are priced as targeted work when the rest of the door is suitable.
  • Powered components: motor, wiring, remote and keypad faults need a clear answer on who performs electrical work.
  • Door condition: age, balance, access and after hours needs can move the final quote inside or outside the guide ranges.

Guide ranges for common repair components

Use the figures as reference points before inspection, not as final prices. The listed service or standard service range is $120 to $200. Torsion spring replacement sits at $220 to $450, lifting cable replacement at $180 to $320, and track roller replacement at $160 to $300. Opener or motor replacement is listed at $600 to $1,100.

Real prices still vary with door type and size, hardware condition, opener brand, site access and after hours surcharges.

  • Ask for the failed component to be named in the quote.
  • Ask whether labour, parts and emergency charges are separated.
  • If the price changes after inspection, ask which condition altered the scope.

When an opener makes the job electrical

A powered opener fault is not the same as a loose remote or a mechanical adjustment. If the motor, wiring or powered opener is involved, the electrical portion should be carried out by a licensed electrician under the Queensland Electrical Safety Act 2002. Separate the symptom from the suspected cause before booking.

  • Say whether the remote has stopped working or the keypad has lost programming.
  • Note whether the motor responds without door movement, or nothing responds.
  • Ask whether the quote includes the motor unit, programming and labour.
  • Check whether existing door hardware suits the proposed opener work.

This keeps the conversation on diagnosis, parts and licensing rather than guesswork.

Repair the component or price a new door

The choice between repair and replacement depends on the failed part and what is found on site. A spring, cable, roller or opener can be a contained repair when balance and operation can be restored. Panel replacement may fit where damage is limited. A new sectional door becomes relevant where repeated faults, poor balance or wider door condition make one part an incomplete fix.

A practical method is to compare one repair range against the $2,500 to $5,000 guide range for a new sectional door. That gives a price anchor before a larger job is approved. It also helps landlords and small property managers keep maintenance decisions consistent across properties.
