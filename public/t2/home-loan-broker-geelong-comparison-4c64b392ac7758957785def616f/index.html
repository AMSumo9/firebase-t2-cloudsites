Borrower scenarios and lender fit

The source page names several borrower scenarios that need specific preparation. First home buyers are directed to work through the Victorian First Home Owner Grant, the First Home Guarantee, LMI and deposit gaps before an inspection weekend or offer deadline compresses the decision. This preparation ensures the lender understands the grant and the deposit source.

Refinancers are told to review the current rate, fees, offset setup, equity and repayment pressure before deciding whether switching lenders is likely to leave them better off. Investors are told to compare interest-only options, offset, an equity-funded deposit and portfolio servicing before a property looks workable on paper but tightens under lender policy.

Common questions

Do mortgage brokers charge fees for home loans? Most residential mortgage brokers in Australia are paid a commission by the lender, so they typically don't charge a direct fee for standard home or investment loans. Some may charge a fee in complex situations, like small loans or unusual circumstances, and should disclose this upfront.

What does a mortgage broker actually do? A mortgage broker assesses your financial situation, compares home loans from different lenders, and recommends suitable options. They help you with the application paperwork, liaise with the lender, and guide you through pre-approval, full approval and settlement. Many also assist with restructuring or refinancing in future if your needs change.
