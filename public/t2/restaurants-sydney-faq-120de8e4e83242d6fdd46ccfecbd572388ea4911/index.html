What review counts can and cannot tell you

Volume is context, not verdict. A larger count can make consistency easier to read; a smaller count can still fit a narrow plan when cuisine, setting and booking details line up. Read the rating beside the profile promise.

  • The Spice Room is listed at 4.6 from 3,889 reviews.
  • Yellow is listed at 4.7 from 362 reviews.
  • China Doll shows 2,210 reviews and is identified as Chinese dining in Woolloomooloo.
  • Matteo Downtown shows 1,693 reviews and is identified as Italian dining in Sydney.
  • Gowings shows 1,182 reviews and is described as a steak house serving premium cuts and classic grilled dishes in a refined setting.

The lesson is not that one number beats another. It is that a steak-house promise, a Woolloomooloo Chinese venue and an Italian city venue serve different nights, so compare fit before rank.
